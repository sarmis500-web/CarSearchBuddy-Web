Privacy

Car Lease Buddy · last updated 12 August 2026

The short version

There is no account, no sign-up and no login, so we never hold your name, email or phone number. We do not sell or share data with advertisers or dealers. Everything below is about measuring how the site is used, so we can make it better.

What we measure

Two standard tools:

  • Google Analytics 4 — counts visits and anonymous interactions: which screens you open, which filters you use, whether a search returned results, and when someone taps through to a dealer's website. Aggregate counts only.
  • Microsoft Clarity — records anonymised session playback and heatmaps (where people tap and scroll, and where the page frustrates them). Clarity masks text input by default, and we never send it anything you type.

Both are loaded only after the page is interactive, and both are optional to your use of the site — if a content blocker stops them, everything still works.

What we never collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or payment details — we never ask for any.
  • Nothing you type into the payment calculator leaves your device. The calculation runs in your browser. We record only that a calculation happened and the rounded shape of it (price, term, rate), never a document you could be identified from.
  • Your precise location is never sent to us or stored. If you allow location access, or type a ZIP code, it is used in your browser to sort cars by distance. We do record the ZIP code you type, so we know which parts of the country to cover better.
  • Saved cars stay in your own browser's storage. They are never uploaded.

Do Not Track

If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, we do not load Microsoft Clarity at all — no session recording takes place. Google Analytics does not check that signal, because it counts visits rather than recording them; to switch it off as well, use any content blocker or your browser's tracking protection, and the site will work exactly the same.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets its own measurement cookies. We set no advertising cookies and run no ad network. The site also uses your browser's local storage to remember saved cars and your settings; that never leaves your device.

Links to dealers

Tapping through to a dealer takes you to a site we do not control, with its own privacy policy. Lease figures shown here are manufacturers' advertised offers and used-car prices are dealer-listed asking prices — always confirm current terms with the dealer.
